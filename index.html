What is a “Legal Notice of Fuck Around and Find Out”?

This is a parody. It is a joke document made to look like a formal legal notice, built around the internet doctrine of Fuck Around and Find Out — universally abbreviated FAFO. It has no legal force whatsoever. There is no firm called Should’ve Listened & Associates, there is no Docket No. FAFO-2026-001, and nobody is actually being served with anything. It exists to be funny.

People use it the way they’d use a gag certificate or a novelty citation: you send it to a friend who ignored good advice and got exactly what was coming to them, you print it and leave it on a coworker’s desk, or you post it when someone in the news spectacularly finds out. It’s a punchline in the shape of a cease-and-desist.

What does FAFO actually mean?

FAFO stands for “Fuck Around and Find Out.” It’s a blunt way of stating cause and effect: the “fuck around” is the reckless or ill-advised action, and the “find out” is the consequence that follows. The phrase caught on because it compresses an entire lecture about consequences into four words. This notice simply dresses that idea up in the language of a courtroom.
